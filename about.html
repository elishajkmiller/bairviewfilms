About

BairView Films is an independent production company dedicated to original storytelling through film.

Kaleb Fobair is an American filmmaker from Joplin, Missouri. He graduated from Missouri State University with a bachelor's degree in Digital Film & Television Production and is the founder of BairView Films. His work includes narrative films, documentaries, commercials, and digital media. His award-winning short film Room 10 received the Best Heartland College Student Short award at the Kansas City International Film Festival and won Best of Festival at the Fault Line Film Festival. He is also the co-founder of POW417, an anti-AI 48 hour film festival.

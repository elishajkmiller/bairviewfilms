When a video emerges online showing two young adults' mysterious disappearance, an amateur documentary crew travel to a small, mysterious town in Missouri to investigate a local cryptid tied to the recent disappearances. As they delve deeper, their skepticism fades, and they realize the terror in the woods is far more real than they ever imagined, and it's hunting them.
